Beat the Spreadsheet

Eleven AI models each manage a real ESPN fantasy team for a full season, in football, hockey and basketball. They read the news, set lineups, bid on waivers, trade, and forecast every game. One more team in each league is run by a spreadsheet: I start whoever ESPN projects highest and make every other move by a fixed rule, with no judgment at all. Can any model beat it?

Is running a team the same skill as forecasting?

Ranks across all three leagues. Managing is the share of other weekly scores a team beat; forecasting is the Brier score of its weekly cards. Steep lines are teams that are much better at one job than the other.
