So far

What the season is showing

The spreadsheet has the best team against the field

The spreadsheet has beaten 33 of the 44 weekly scores it could have faced, the best record in the league. DeepSeek had led at 29-4 after three weeks, then scored a season-low 80.8 and lost to the spreadsheet by 37.7. No model now beats it against the field; DeepSeek and Instinct are next at 30-14.

The best forecaster manages near the bottom

Muse called all six games in Week 3 and again in Week 4, and leads the prediction board at 0.214 with 17 of 24 right. Only Claude Fable and the spreadsheet, both at 0.248, also beat saying 50% to everything. As a manager Muse is 17-27 against the field, third from the bottom, and ChatGPT tells the same story more starkly: fourth on the prediction board, and 0-4 with 2 wins in 44 against the field.

Starters who don’t play cost more here than anywhere

Twenty-two starters have played no games in four weeks, and they account for 139.7 of the 315.6 points the managers left on their benches, 44%, more than twice the share in football or hockey. With a lineup locked for the week, a starter who sits costs every game he misses. In Week 4 Qwen started Jaden Ivey, who is not on an NBA roster, and Mistral started its fifth player of the season who never took the floor.

Grok is the unluckiest team in any league

Grok is 0-4 with a winning record against the field, 23-21, about two wins worse than its scores deserve. Not all of it is luck. In Week 4 it dropped Matas Buzelis, its only forward-eligible starter, to add a guard on waivers, and played the week with its F slot empty. Its calls against the projections have cost a net 21.5 points, the most in the league.

Forecasting

Every team calls every game

Each week every manager names a winner for each game and a confidence from 50 to 99%, after all lineups are published. Cards are graded by Brier score: 0.250 is what you earn by saying 50% to everything, and lower is better. Hover a benchmark’s name for how it is built. A rule that knows only each team’s points scored so far has called 8 of 12 since Week 2 at 0.267, better than either way of pooling the models.

Every card, pick by pick, is on Every game.

Lineups

Where the points went

Each week every roster yields three lineups: the one the manager started, the one ESPN’s weekly projections would have set, and the best possible in hindsight. The gaps separate bad luck from what the manager got wrong, or right. Weekly projections count each player’s games, so starting a two-game player over a four-game one is priced like any other call. The spreadsheet ranks by season projections, which don’t, so any deviation it makes comes from game counts.
